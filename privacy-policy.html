Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Nudasys is a greeting-card studio for chocolate-themed frames, stickers, and wishes. This policy describes what the app handles on your device.

Where data lives

Photos you capture or import, drafts, finished PNG cards, sample cards, and Card Credits stay in the app’s private storage on this device. Nudasys does not operate an account server and does not upload your cards, photos, or wishes.

Account

Log In is one tap on this device. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. The app stores a local session so the next launch can open the studio. Log Out clears that session only. Your cards and Card Credits remain on the device, and you must log in again to see them. Delete Account removes the local account and the drafts, copied photos, finished cards, and Card Credits stored for it. The next log in starts a new account on this device. Neither action sends data to Nudasys.

What you create

A card can include pictures you choose, a frame, a collage layout, sticker positions, colors, and the wish text you type or confirm after dictation. Rendering a card creates a PNG in the app. Saving to Photos writes a copy only after you tap Save to Photos. Sample cards shown in the studio are local examples, not a live feed from other people.

Permissions

  • Camera: requested when you tap Take Photo, to capture one still picture for the card.
  • Photo library: requested when you tap Import from Photos, to read pictures you select.
  • Add to Photos: requested when you tap Save to Photos, to add the finished PNG.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: requested when you tap Dictate Wishes, to turn speech into text in the message field. You confirm that text before it is kept. Nudasys does not store the audio.

Nudasys does not ask to track you across other companies’ apps and does not send notification alerts.

Purchases

Card Credits packs are consumable in-app purchases. Apple charges your Apple ID. A verified purchase adds credits on this device. A new account also receives a small local starter balance that is not an Apple product. Rendering one greeting card uses 1 Card Credit after you confirm. Credits are not restored if you delete the account, delete the app, or reinstall. Refunds are handled by Apple. If Apple revokes a purchase, Nudasys removes only the unused portion of that grant. Finished cards that were already rendered stay until you delete them.
