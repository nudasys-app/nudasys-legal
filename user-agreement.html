User Agreement

Effective 13 October 2026

This agreement covers your use of Nudasys, an app for making chocolate-themed greeting cards from photos you provide.

The studio

You may take or import photos, choose a frame and collage, place stickers, write a wish, and render a PNG. Frames, stickers, and wish samples are part of the studio. Nudasys does not send the card for you and does not publish it.

Account

You log in with one tap on this device. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. Log Out ends the session and keeps your cards and Card Credits on the device. Delete Account removes that local account and the cards, drafts, photos, and Card Credits stored for it. Deletion cannot be undone. The next log in is a new account.

Your content

You are responsible for the photos and words you put on a card. Keep copies you care about outside the app. Delete Account removes the copies stored for that account on this device.

Card Credits

Optional consumable packs are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple charges your Apple ID. A successful render of a new card uses 1 Card Credit after you confirm the cost. Saving or sharing that same finished card does not use another credit. Credits stay on this device. They are not restored after Delete Account, or after you delete the app. Refunds are requested from Apple.

Permissions

The camera, photo library, and microphone are used only after you tap the matching control. You can refuse and still type a wish or use the other photo path.
